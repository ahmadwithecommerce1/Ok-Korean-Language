Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

OK Korean Language Center (https://www.ok-korean.online/) respects user privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of our students, parents/guardians, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard your information when you enroll in our courses or interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an admission request, book a trial class, or enroll in a program, we collect the following information:

  • Personal Identity Details: Student full name (English and optional Korean name) and father/guardian name.
  • Contact Information: Student contact phone number, parent WhatsApp number, and email address (optional).
  • Enrollment & Payment Details: Assigned batch, enrollment date, academic notes, and tuition payment records required for course administration and billing.

3. How We Use Information (Including WhatsApp Notifications)

We use the collected information strictly for educational administration, student support, and official institute communication, including:

  • WhatsApp Alerts & Academic Updates: With your explicit opt-in consent, we send daily attendance alerts, mock test & TOPIK scores, class schedule changes, and fee reminders directly via WhatsApp Business API.
  • Enrollment Management: Assigning batches, processing course admissions, and maintaining student academic records.
  • Student Support: Responding to trial bookings, inquiries, and visa/EPS-TOPIK documentation guidance.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

OK Korean Language Center does NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

We may share necessary contact information (such as your name and phone number) solely with WhatsApp / Meta Platforms and our authorized messaging infrastructure providers exclusively to deliver verified attendance alerts, test scores, and fee reminders that you have opted in to receive.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and administrative security measures—including encrypted HTTPS connections, access-controlled cloud databases, and restricted staff permissions—to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.
